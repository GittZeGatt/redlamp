Highlights:

- **The grid and the loupe**: Redlamp now has a Library beside Develop, in the same window, as Lightroom Classic does. Its grid shows thumbnails from 80 to 400 points in three cell styles, with click, ⌘-click, ⇧-click and rubber-band selection, and each folder or collection keeps its size, place and selection; the loupe shows one photo at Fit or 1:1 ([#227](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/227), [#228](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/228)).
- **Culling with Auto Advance**: ratings, flags, colour labels, your own labels and marks reach every selected photo at once, with Undo, by key, by a click on a cell or from the menus. With Auto Advance on, each one moves on to the next photo ([#229](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/229)).
- **Compare and Survey**: C compares the photo you're keeping with the next candidate, side by side with zoom and pan in step, and N lays out a selection as large as it fits. Held arrow keys move through the grid, the loupe and Develop without a blank frame ([#230](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/230)).
- **Collections and smart collections**: collections and sets, saved in each photo's sidecar so rebuilding the library loses none, and smart collections that stay current as saved searches ([#237](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/237)).
- **Search**: a search field always on screen, its terms shown as chips, searching what's shown or the whole library with Search All (⌘K); filters by attributes and by up to eight metadata columns with counts, and seven sorts ([#232](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/232)).
- **Import**: File › Import Photos… (⇧⌘I) opens when a card goes in. Browse it at once from its previews, grouped by moment, cull before copying, and copy to a destination and a backup, every copy checked before the card is safe to erase; photos already imported are left out ([#241](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/241)).
- **Polish and your theme**: the filter bar, the grid's cells and bars, the sidebar, the panels and the import window reworked with macOS's own controls, and the library in Redlamp's theme, as Develop is ([#328](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/328)).
