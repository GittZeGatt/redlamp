The eye at the right of every Develop panel's header but Basic's switches the panel off and on, a step in History each time. A panel that's off keeps its settings, dimmed, and does nothing to the photo: Detail off is no sharpening or noise reduction. Changing a setting turns it back on, and the switch travels with the panel's settings when they're copied, pasted or synced. A panel's Edited chip counts the kinds of setting it has changed, as Copy Settings lists them, and names them on hover.

In the picture, Detail is off, its Sharpening and Noise Reduction kept.
