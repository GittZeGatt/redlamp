With several photos selected, Mask Presets heads its presets *Apply to N Selected Photos*, and a preset goes to every one, its AI masks made for each, as Paste does: a step in the open photo's history, and a Settings Sync for the others that Undo Sync Settings takes back. The filmstrip says how many were left alone, and why. Landscape, from New Mask, Add, Subtract or Intersect, opens a picker of the regions SAM 3 finds, each with its share of the photo, to mask together or one each.

In the picture, the Landscape picker on a photo of a cove, with three photos selected.
