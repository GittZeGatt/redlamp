New Mask, and Add, Subtract and Intersect on a mask, open one picker for every kind of mask. A menu button sits on each component and on the mask selected or under the pointer, and Invert at the top of a mask's settings inverts all of it. Rows show a thumbnail of their mask's coverage, the photo previews the mask, component or pin under the pointer, even with the overlay off, and pins sit where each mask covers most. People opens a picker of who is in the photo, as crops to tick, with the parts to mask.

In the picture, a car's Subject mask selected, beside a Sky mask and a gradient.
