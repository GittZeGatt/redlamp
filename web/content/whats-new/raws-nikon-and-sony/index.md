Nikon's High Efficiency NEFs, HE and HE\*, open from the Z 9, Z 8, Z f, Z 6III, Z5 II and Z50 II, through a decoder [Redlamp's fork of LibRaw](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp-libraw) carries and checks on CC0 samples. Other bodies' HE files, the ZR's among them, are refused until a sample of theirs is verified, and the Z5 II's and Z50 II's still open without a colour matrix. The fork is built on LibRaw's master branch, which opens the Sony A7 V, and the A1 II's YCbCr ARWs without a black strip along the bottom, though with a magenta cast.

In the picture, a Z 8's HE\* file in Redlamp.
